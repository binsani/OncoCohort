# Intended Use and Product Boundary

## Intended use

OncoCohort is an administrative research workspace intended to help authorized oncology research teams:

- define and organize research cohorts
- record synthetic or institutionally governed cohort data
- track enrollment and consent-administration status
- review operational counts and activity history
- export authorized research-workspace records

## Intended users

Trained clinical researchers, study coordinators, data managers, principal investigators, and authorized administrators.

## Explicitly excluded uses

OncoCohort is not intended to:

- diagnose, screen, prevent, monitor, predict, prognose, treat, or alleviate disease
- recommend patient-specific treatment or eligibility decisions
- replace professional medical judgment
- control or drive a medical device
- serve as the authoritative electronic health record
- store identifiable health data before organizational approval

The current software displays and organizes information entered by users. It does not calculate clinical risk or generate a patient-specific medical recommendation.

## Deployment boundary

Each deploying organization is responsible for approving its users, lawful basis, consent model, data minimization, retention, incident response, backups, vendor review, and applicable regulatory obligations.

## Change-control warning

Adding clinical recommendations, diagnostic logic, predictive models, automated eligibility decisions, or device integrations may change the regulatory classification. Such changes require a documented regulatory review before development or release.
