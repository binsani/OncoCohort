# Regulatory Pathway — Nigeria

This document is a planning aid, not legal advice or regulatory approval.

## Current product position

The current intended use is administrative oncology research cohort management. It expressly excludes diagnosis, prediction, prognosis, treatment recommendations, and automated clinical decisions.

## Classification decision

Before commercialization or clinical deployment, obtain a written classification opinion from a qualified Nigerian medical-device regulatory professional and, where appropriate, NAFDAC.

If the intended purpose becomes a medical purpose, NAFDAC's Software as a Medical Device registration guideline should be assessed. The evidence package may need to address:

- device description and intended purpose
- risk classification
- quality management and design controls
- software lifecycle and configuration management
- verification and validation
- cybersecurity
- clinical evaluation and performance evidence
- labeling and instructions
- post-market surveillance, vigilance, and change control

## Data protection

Separately from medical-device classification, the deploying organization must assess obligations under Nigeria's data-protection framework, including health-data safeguards and any required Data Protection Impact Assessment.

## Official starting points

- NAFDAC, Guidelines for Registration of Software as a Medical Device in Nigeria, effective 1 July 2024
- NAFDAC medical-device quality, registration, vigilance, and variation guidance
- Nigeria Data Protection Commission guidance and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
- IMDRF Software as a Medical Device clinical-evaluation principles

## Approval gate

Do not describe OncoCohort as approved, certified, compliant, or suitable for production clinical use until the responsible authority or qualified assessor has issued the relevant written determination.
